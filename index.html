A twin-turbo inline-six, three transmissions, and one unmistakable silhouette — the M4 is BMW's argument for the driver's car.

S58 Inline-Six 473–523 HP RWD or xDrive

The engine is the argument.

The S58's twin-turbo inline-six climbs from 473 to 523 horsepower depending on trim, wrapped in a chassis tuned to put every bit of it down.
